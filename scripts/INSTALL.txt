MacWall — personal video desktop wallpaper for macOS
Created by @mohind

INSTALL
  1. Drag MacWall.app onto the Applications folder (right here in this window).
  2. Open MacWall from Applications.
  3. First launch only: if macOS says it "can't be opened because Apple cannot
     check it for malicious software", right-click (Control-click) MacWall.app
     -> Open -> Open. (The app is code-signed, but not notarized by Apple.)

REQUIREMENTS
  - Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer)
  - macOS 13 or newer

USE
  - MacWall lives in the menu bar (no Dock icon).
  - Menu bar icon -> Open Dashboard... to pick your videos:
      WALLPAPER    - the video loops on your desktop, behind the icons
      SCREENSAVER  - full-screen video while the Mac is idle
      LOCK SCREEN  - a full-res still frame of the video on the lock screen
  - Add Videos... (or drag & drop): your files stay exactly where they are —
    nothing is copied, moved or uploaded.
  - Start at Login (dashboard Settings) brings the wallpaper back after a restart.

NOTES
  - No account, no network. Everything is local.
  - macOS does not allow apps to play video on the real lock screen (only
    Apple's own wallpapers can animate there) — the lock screen shows a
    full-resolution frame of your chosen video instead.
  - Uninstall: quit from the menu bar, delete MacWall.app, and remove the
    login item if enabled (dashboard -> Start at Login off).
